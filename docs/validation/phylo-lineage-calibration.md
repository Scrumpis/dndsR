# Lineage-level methods: calibration in simulation

Results of the study designed in [dev/phylo-lineage-calibration.md](../dev/phylo-lineage-calibration.md), judged by the criteria fixed there before any simulation was analysed. **Simulation only**: the data were generated by a codon model with known truth, so these numbers say how the methods behave when their assumptions roughly hold, not how often they are right on real genomes.

## Run

- dndsR at `dcf3231`, installed into a frozen library; v0.4.0 image (R 4.6.2); PAML 4.10.10 and HyPhy 2.5.103 from Bioconda. Driver `tools/dev/validation/phylo-lineage-calibration.sb`, simulator `phylo-lineage-sim.R`, summary `phylo-lineage-calibration-summary.R` (committed at `f7aeaea`, before any result was read).
- Slurm array of 27 units, 8 cores each: 27 of 27 completed, 0 genes failed in any unit, 234.6 core-hours (limit 250). Seeds are the array index, per unit. RELAX ran with `models = "Minimal"`; aBSREL tested every branch; branch models used the defaults (three starting omegas).
- Results root outside the repository; the summary tables below are copied from `<root>/summary/*.tsv`.

**Verdicts.** The design defines three: *calibrated* (upper 95% Clopper-Pearson bound at most 0.05), *consistent with nominal* (estimate at most 0.05, upper bound above), *anticonservative* (lower bound above 0.05). Rates above 0.05 whose interval still includes 0.05 fit none of the three; the summary script, committed before the analysis, labels them *above nominal, not significantly*, and they are reported as such here. With 300 genes, a rate must be at most about 2.3% for its upper bound to reach 0.05, so *consistent with nominal* is the best a test exactly at its level can get.

Reported, not judged (100 genes per cell; 95% intervals).

| Unit | Test | Detected |

| A1 T5 / T8b / T16 | `p_two_vs_m0`, foreground 0.6 vs background 0.2 | 98% / 99% / 100% |
| A4 T8b | `p_fg_omega_gt1`, foreground 1.5 at every site | 78% (69-86) |
| B1 T8b / T16 | aBSREL `p_holm`, 10% of sites at omega 10 on one terminal branch | 79% / 72% |
| B1 T8b / T16 | aBSREL `p_bh_lineage`, same branch | 93% / 96% |
| C1 T8b / T16 | RELAX, k = 0.5 (relaxation) | 59% / 62%; k < 1 in 100% / 98% of detections |
| C2 T8b / T16 | RELAX, k = 2 (intensification) | 38% / 35%; k > 1 in 95% / 100% of detections |

The per-lineage correction is more powerful than the within-gene Holm correction for a branch named in advance, as it should be: it corrects across genes on one branch rather than across branches in one gene. RELAX detects moderate relaxation in about 60% of genes and moderate intensification in about a third; when significant, the direction of `k` was almost always right.

- **The flags meet the criterion where they fire**: flagged estimates are off by 3 to 12 log2 units (8- to 5,000-fold), unflagged ones by 0.34-0.52 (1.3- to 1.4-fold). But they fire on few branches (at most 2.5%, on the pectinate tree's shortest and longest), and **one unflagged per-gene, per-branch estimate in five is still more than 2-fold off**. A single free-ratio omega is noisy even when unflagged; it is for exploration, and lineages should be compared through pooled values.
- Two-ratio estimates are tighter: median error 0.17-0.21 for the foreground and 0.10-0.14 for the background (A1, A4).

Median absolute log2 error over branches, against the branch's true omega, of three summaries over 100 genes:

| Tree | Pooled omega (`phylo_summarize`) | Median of reliable per-gene estimates | Mean of all per-gene estimates |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| T8b (12 branches) | 0.044 (max 0.15) | 0.071 (max 0.17) | 0.143 (max 0.31) |
| T8p (12) | 0.081 (max 0.19) | 0.096 (max 0.47) | 0.294 (max 2.26) |
| T16 (28) | 0.058 (max 0.15) | 0.086 (max 0.22) | 0.195 (max 0.70) |

The pooled omega is within about 3-6% of the truth on a typical branch and within 15% on the worst, better than the median of per-gene values, and far better than the mean of per-gene ratios, which is biased upwards and on one short pectinate branch is off almost 5-fold (0.44 against a true 0.09). This supports summing substitutions rather than averaging ratios.

## What this establishes, and what it does not

- **Calibrated in this simulation** (the size criterion was met on every tree tried): aBSREL's `p_raw`, gene `p_value`, and `p_bh_lineage`; Benjamini-Hochberg-corrected branch-model tests; `p_fg_omega_gt1` on genes under purifying selection.
- **Close to nominal, not significantly above it**: `p_two_vs_m0` and `p_free_vs_m0` with complete taxon sampling (rates up to 7%), and RELAX on 5 and 8 taxa.
- **Anticonservative**: `p_free_vs_m0` with missing taxa (8% on T16m) and **RELAX on 16 taxa (9%)**. Documented as limitations.
- **Not measured**: the size of `p_fg_omega_gt1` at a foreground omega of 1; anything about real genomes (alignment and orthology errors, multinucleotide substitutions, recombination, gene-tree discordance), which in the BUSTED calibration were the main source of false positives; trees other than these five; genes other than 300 codons.

All four commands remain **experimental**. Their tests are reasonably calibrated under the model, and their estimates are accurate when pooled; whether conclusions drawn from them on real genomes are right is not something simulation can show.
